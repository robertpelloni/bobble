# OmniCore Memory

Ongoing internal architectural observations, codebase traits, and design preferences.

- Core Engine: Rust workspace containing `omnicore-engine` (headless daemon & protocol bridges) and `omnicore-ui` (Tauri desktop app).
- Mobile Shell: React Native + Expo app in `omnicore-mobile/` for iOS and Android, interfacing with the engine daemon over local IPC / WebSocket.
- Protocol Bridges (`omnicore-engine/src/bridges/`):
  - Matrix: `matrix-sdk` integration with homeserver session and sync token storage.
  - Signal: Unix socket IPC to `signald` daemon.
  - Discord: `serenity` REST / gateway client.
  - SimpleX: Unidirectional isolated cryptographic queue broker routing (`simplex.rs`).
  - Tox: DHT bootstrapping and peer-to-peer friend dispatch (`tox.rs`).
  - SMS: Serial AT cellular modem transport for offline fallback (`sms.rs`).
  - iMessage: BlueBubbles REST API and AppleScript relay client (`imessage.rs`).
- Bridge Integration: `BridgeRegistry` and `MultipathRouter` in `omnicore-engine/src/router.rs` managing dynamic multi-bridge dispatch with automated cascade fallback.
- Federation & Multi-Party: `FederationManager` and `VectorClock` in `omnicore-engine/src/federation.rs` enabling decentralized group channel replication.
- Cloud Backup: `ZeroKnowledgeBackup` in `omnicore-engine/src/backup.rs` managing client-side encrypted chunking and manifest generation.
- Decentralized Identity: `IdentityCard` and `IdentityFingerprint` in `omnicore-engine/src/identity.rs` providing exportable, verifiable cryptographic identity cards.
- Privacy & Anonymity: `TorManager` in `omnicore-engine/src/tor.rs` managing SOCKS5 proxy circuits and v3 .onion hidden service endpoints.
- Outbox & Ratchets: `OutboxManager` and `RatchetState` in `omnicore-engine/src/outbox.rs` ensuring offline message queue persistence and forward secrecy key progression.
- Hardware Appliance: `TelemetryCollector` in `omnicore-engine/src/telemetry.rs` monitoring battery status, thermal throttling, and power source failovers for standalone operation.
- VoIP & Calling: `CallSession` in `omnicore-engine/src/call.rs` implementing cross-protocol WebRTC signaling and SDP negotiation.
- Local Discovery: `PeerDiscovery` in `omnicore-engine/src/discovery.rs` generating and parsing UDP beacons for zero-server LAN synchronization.
- Domain Models: Explicit types in `omnicore-engine/src/models.rs` for `ProtocolKind`, `UnifiedContact`, `ProtocolMapping`, `ConversationMessage`, `DeliveryState`, and `RoutingStrategy`.
- Encrypted Storage: SQLCipher with AES-256-GCM encryption (`cipher_page_size=4096`, `kdf_iter=256000`, `foreign_keys=ON`). Schema includes `contacts`, `mappings`, `threads`, `messages`, `attachments`, `key_store`, and `sync_tokens`. Complete design documented in `omnicore-engine/SCHEMA.md`.
- Dispatcher: Multi-path timeout fallback engine in `omnicore-engine/src/dispatcher.rs` executing concurrent queues and automated protocol fallback.
- Desktop UI: React 19 + TypeScript + Vite + TailwindCSS running inside Tauri v2 shell with system tray controls.
- Global Versioning: Centrally tracked in `VERSION.md` (currently 0.8.8).
- Cross-Protocol Packet Fragmentation & MTU Adaptor: `ProtocolMtuLimits`, `PayloadFragmenter`, and `MultipathRouter::route_with_fragmentation_adaptive_cascade` in `omnicore-engine/src/router.rs` automatically chunk oversized message payloads when cascading to constrained transports like SMS or Tox.
- Mesh Relay Bridge Gateway Adapter: `MeshRelayBridgeAdapter` in `omnicore-engine/src/mesh.rs` bridges local P2P mesh packets to external protocol bridges (`MultipathRouter::route_with_cascade`), allowing offline mesh nodes to egress packets to internet/cellular networks via gateway nodes.
- Cross-Protocol Recipient Translation: `CrossProtocolAddressBook` and `MultipathRouter::route_unified_message` in `omnicore-engine/src/router.rs` translate unified contact identifiers to protocol-specific destination addresses at each cascade step.
- Penalty-Weighted Route Selection: `MultipathRouter::route_with_penalty_weighted_cascade` and `ProtocolMetric::calculate_route_penalty_score` in `omnicore-engine/src/router.rs` dynamically compute combined latency and failure penalties to deprioritize degraded bridges before circuit tripping.
- High-Throughput Batch Event Broadcast: `FederationManager::broadcast_batch_events_via_router` in `omnicore-engine/src/federation.rs` bounds outbound concurrency chunks to mitigate rate-limiting and buffer bloat under heavy loads.
- Flapping Network Resilience & Exponential Cooldown: `MultipathRouter::route_with_flapping_resilience` in `omnicore-engine/src/router.rs` prioritizes non-flapping candidates and applies exponential backoff on circuit cooldowns for flapping bridges.
- Adaptive Fallback Retry Router: `MultipathRouter::route_with_adaptive_fallback_retry` in `omnicore-engine/src/router.rs` executes multi-pass delivery with circuit breaker half-open auto-recovery across passes.
- Multi-Bridge Mesh Hedged Broadcast: `FederationManager::broadcast_event_via_hedged_racing` in `omnicore-engine/src/federation.rs` races candidate bridges concurrently when distributing signed events to decentralized multi-party group channels.
- Hedged Multi-Path Racing: `MultipathRouter::route_hedged_racing` in `omnicore-engine/src/router.rs` races primary and fallback bridges concurrently or with a configurable tail-latency cutoff (`HedgedRoutingPolicy`) to minimize latency under packet loss.
- Circuit Cooldown Jitter: `ProtocolMetric::with_jitter` adds dynamic jitter to circuit breaker cooldowns, preventing thundering herd reconnect storms across flapping networks.
- Hardware Video Decoder: `omnicore-engine/src/call.rs` implements `HardwareVideoDecoder` supporting V4L2 M2M, NVDEC, VideoToolbox, and software OpenH264 fallback with IDR keyframe synchronization.
- Replay Attack Protection: `omnicore-engine/src/router.rs` implements `NonceReplayCache` and `RoutedPacketEnvelope` ensuring nonces cannot be replayed across alternate routing bridges.
- Hardware Video Encoder: `omnicore-engine/src/call.rs` implements `HardwareVideoEncoder` supporting VA-API, VideoToolbox, NVENC, and software OpenH264 fallback with NALU compression.
- Video Color Conversion: `omnicore-engine/src/call.rs` implements `VideoColorConverter` for box spatial downsampling and ITU-R BT.601 RGBA to Planar I420 color space conversion.
- Zero-Copy Video Frame Pool: `omnicore-engine/src/call.rs` implements `VideoFramePool` managing pre-allocated reusable `RawVideoFrame` memory buffers for I420, NV12, and RGBA32 formats to eliminate GC allocation overhead during video encoding.
- Payload Integrity Verification: `omnicore-engine/src/router.rs` implements `PayloadIntegrityGuard` and `MultipathRouter::route_with_integrity_check` verifying payload integrity before and after cascading dispatch.
- Encrypted Screen Sharing Pipeline: `omnicore-engine/src/call.rs` implements `ScreenSharePipeline` managing MTU chunking, integrity checksums, out-of-order chunk assembly, and session encryption keys for screen video streams.
- Video Simulcast Controller: `omnicore-engine/src/call.rs` implements `VideoSimulcastController` adjusting video spatial resolutions (1080p, 480p, 240p) and frame rates (30fps, 24fps, 15fps) across High, Medium, and Low simulcast layers.
- Starvation Prevention: `MultipathRouter::route_with_starvation_prevention` dynamically promotes unattempted (0 attempts) bridges to probe status during primary latency spikes.
- Bridge Health Anomaly Detection: `omnicore-engine/src/telemetry.rs` implements `BridgeHealthMonitor` and `RoutingAnomaly` for diagnosing latency spikes, repeated failures, circuit locks, and starvation bottlenecks across routing bridges.
- Dynamic Bitrate Controller: `omnicore-engine/src/call.rs` implements `BitrateController` for adjusting Opus audio stream bit-rates and frame duration (20ms/40ms) based on real-time packet loss and round-trip latency metrics.
- Bridge Hardening: `IMessageBridge` and `SmsBridge` validate non-empty inputs, provide connection readiness queries (`is_connected`, `is_ready`), and implement configurable retry loops.
- Mesh Cache Pruning & Daemon: `omnicore-engine/src/mesh.rs` implements `MeshRouter::prune_expired_seen` preventing memory leaks and `MeshTransportDaemon` coordinating attached BLE/Wi-Fi Direct peer channels.
- RTP Jitter Buffer & PLC: `omnicore-engine/src/call.rs` implements `RtpJitterBuffer` for sorting out-of-order media packets, suppressing duplicates, enforcing playout delays, tracking sequence loss, and synthesizing comfort-noise PLC frames.
- Optimal Cascade Routing: `MultipathRouter::route_with_optimal_cascade` dynamically optimizes candidate order, bypasses open circuit bridges, and records real-time latency and delivery outcomes.
- Multi-Hop Mesh Routing: `omnicore-engine/src/mesh.rs` implements store-and-forward mesh packet queueing, cyclic loop prevention, and deduplication across BLE and Wi-Fi Direct transports.
- Cross-Protocol Call Gateway: `omnicore-engine/src/call.rs` implements `CrossProtocolCallGateway` bridging WebRTC, Matrix VoIP, and Discord voice calls with RTP sequence translation.
- Hardware Appliance Routing: `omnicore-engine/src/telemetry.rs` implements `ApplianceConfig` to trigger cellular LTE modem failover during power outages and execute thermal/battery safe shutdowns.
